Website Types We Build

Different businesses need different solutions. Here are the primary website types we specialize in:

Service Portals & Booking Sites

Appointment scheduling, booking calendars, service selection tools, and client dashboards. Clients can book, reschedule, and track their service requests without email back-and-forth.

Build a Following (Mailing List & Community)

Content hubs with email list signup, newsletter automation, community messaging, and subscriber-only resources. Perfect for creators, coaches, and thought leaders building an audience.

Gallery and Portfolio Sites

Beautiful, responsive galleries for artists, photographers, designers, and makers. Image-heavy layouts, lightbox galleries, filtering, search, and seamless e-commerce integration for selling work.

Storefronts and E-Commerce

Product catalogs, shopping carts, payment processing, inventory tracking, and order management. From simple single-product sites to multi-category stores with real business operations.

Brochure and Company Sites

Professional presence for service businesses, consultants, and agencies. Clean design, team bios, case studies, service descriptions, and lead capture forms.

Hybrid Multi-Function Platforms

Combinations of the above: a gallery with e-commerce, a booking portal with a newsletter, a storefront with a community. We handle complex, integrated workflows.

Integration and Workflow

Pricing Complexity and Scope Considerations

Website costs vary dramatically based on what you need. Here's what moves a project from simple to complex:

Simple (Lower Cost)

Single-page or 3-5 page brochure site. Template-based, no custom functionality, minimal design customization.

Moderate (Mid Cost)

5-15 pages, some custom design, basic contact forms, SEO optimization, simple blog or content management.

Complex (Higher Cost)

Multi-page with custom functionality: booking systems, e-commerce, user accounts, payment processing, integrations.

Very Complex (Enterprise Cost)

Database-driven, real-time updates, custom workflows, advanced security, third-party integrations, ongoing optimization.

What drives cost up: User accounts and authentication, payment and transaction processing, database design and management, custom integrations (CRM, accounting, email), inventory management, multi-language support, custom analytics, role-based content control, real-time notifications.
