Support Plan Boundaries

Scope of Regular Services Provided

Hourly, weekly and monthly support plans are for improvement, support and administration of existing IT infrastructure - not intended for engineering work related to design or delivery of any novel equipment or piece of software.

  • Custom design/build work is moved to engineering project scope
  • No support-hour conversion into engineering deliverables
  • Major redesign work requires separate statement of work (SOW) approval

Cleveland area on-site or remote service, transparent scope boundaries, and a free 15-minute Zoom intro call before paid technical consultation.

Service Agreement Highlights

Deposits and Milestones

Projects up to $3,000 use 50% kickoff and 50% delivery billing. Larger projects use milestone billing (40% kickoff, 30% midpoint, 30% delivery).

Payment Terms

Weekly invoices are net 7 days. Monthly support invoices are net 10 days. Project milestone invoices are due on issue.

Late Payments

Friendly reminder at 5 days overdue, late fee after 10 days, and active support pauses on accounts 30+ days past due.

Monthly Hard Cap Rule

Monthly support includes up to 24 hours with availability capped at 28 hours. Hours 25-28 require written approval and are billed at the standard hourly rate.

Scope Change Process

New requirements are handled through a written change order showing added scope, estimated effort, and updated price before work begins.

Support vs Engineering

Support plans cover maintenance/admin operations. Engineering design, custom build/test, and formal deliverables require a separate project SOW.
