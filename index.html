Our story

Where engineering meets play

VSITEdesigns is the maker-studio spinoff of TES, LLC (Tevis Engineering Services). Where TES brings the precision and problem-solving, this studio channels it into playful, tangible art you can put on a shelf.

Every map, statue, and custom piece is modeled and printed in-house here in Ohio. We love celebrating local landmarks — the skylines, the stadiums, and the rolling topography of the Buckeye State and beyond.
