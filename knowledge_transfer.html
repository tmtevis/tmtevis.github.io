Our Engagement Approach

Every knowledge transfer project is scoped, planned, and delivered as a formal statement of work. We work from your source materials and expertise to deliver a finished knowledge asset.

Discovery and Scoping

We identify knowledge gaps, target audience, and format preferences. You point us to the people and systems that hold the knowledge.

Knowledge Capture

We conduct interviews, observe workflows, review existing materials, and extract institutional knowledge in a structured way.

Organization and Structure

We organize captured information into a logical, searchable structure appropriate to your business model and user roles.

Format and Hosting Decision

We recommend hosting options (wiki, intranet, knowledge base platform, dashboard, etc.) and implement your preferred solution.

Documentation Creation

We create, format, and link all documentation into a cohesive whole. Internal review and feedback cycles keep quality high.

Delivery and Training

We deliver the complete knowledge asset, train your team on maintenance, and provide handoff documentation.

Intangible Benefits

Reduced Single-Person Risk

When key knowledge is documented and accessible, your organization is no longer dependent on any one person's availability or memory.

Faster Onboarding

New team members ramp up faster when they have a clear, searchable reference instead of learning through trial and error or repeated interruptions.

Consistent Quality

Documented processes reduce variation and ensure work is delivered to standard regardless of who performs it.

Easier Change Management

When you update a process, you have a single source of truth to update instead of trying to track down all the different places knowledge lives.

Client and Stakeholder Confidence

Organizations that can show a mature, documented approach to their work gain trust from clients, regulators, and investors.

Longevity and Succession Planning

Your intellectual property becomes a transferable asset. Leadership transitions and staff changes become less disruptive.

Scope and Pricing

Every knowledge transfer engagement is unique. Pricing depends on scope: breadth of knowledge, complexity of format, hosting platform choice, and depth of training.

Typical projects range from focused documentation of a single critical workflow to a comprehensive organizational knowledge base. We scope and price via statement of work once we understand your goals and constraints.
